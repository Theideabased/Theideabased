### Contrast

Foreground, muted text, primary-button labels, focus rings, and borders were derived to meet WCAG AA in both modes. Keep body copy on solid background or card tokens; never place body copy directly on the accent gradient.

## Typography — Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display:** Instrument Serif, for hero statements and editorial emphasis
- **Body and UI:** Inter
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono, for outcomes, metrics, and technical labels

Wired through `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx`. CSS variables are exposed through Tailwind as `font-serif`, `font-sans`, and `font-mono`.

## Gradients

Two gradients are derived from Acid Thesis:

- `--gradient-bg`: a subtle hero background, used only for large atmospheric areas
- `--gradient-accent`: a growth-green accent treatment, reserved for small brand moments

Do not stack the gradients or use either behind long-form text.

## Tone and voice

### Use

Direct, specific, confident, and number-forward language. Lead with outcomes, then explain the thinking that produced them. Use short sentences and active verbs. Let restraint signal confidence.

### Avoid

Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “cutting-edge,” “unlock,” “seamless,” and generic claims about innovation. Do not hide meaning behind technical jargon or stack adjectives.

### Voice example

> I do not fit neatly in a job title. That is the point. I build the product, the system behind it, and the momentum that helps it grow.

## Usage

**Do:** use semantic tokens, maintain generous whitespace, keep one primary action per surface, use mono numerals for metrics, and test both themes at 375 px, 768 px, and 1280 px.

**Do not:** hardcode component colors, use `transition: all`, create uniform card grids, overuse gradients, or replace concrete results with vague claims.
